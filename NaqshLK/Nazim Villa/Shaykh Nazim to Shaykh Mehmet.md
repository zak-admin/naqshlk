From his fathers side he is a descendant of Gouz ul 'Alam, Sheikh Abdul Qadir Jilani.

From his mothers side, he is a descendant of the famous Sufi Saint, Mevlana Jalaluddin Rumi.

##### Shaykh Nazim: Interest and Direction towards Religious Knowledge

Young Sheikh Nazim at 4 years old, would be found in the circles of learned Scholars and Imams, at their Dhikrs or when discoursing religious matters at Umm Hiram Mosque.

In 1940 Sheikh Nazim was sent to the University of Istanbul where 3 of his brothers studied too. He obtained a degree in Chemical Engineering excelling his colleagues and was known as a genius.

Suddenly one of his brothers, whom he loved so dearly, go killed at the 2nd  World War. That brother was a Doctor and was needed at the War.

This deeply saddened Sheikh Nazim and made him realise further, that this life is only temporary.

He then left the worldy life and went in search of Saints and the knowledge of Allah Almighty.

He began learning from many eminent Sheikhs, Scholars and Saints but at a point they could no longer teach him further.

His capacity was too high, therefore he could surpass his teachres too soon.

##### Shaykh Nazim's direction towards his Real Shaykh

One day Sheikh Nazim was told that a great Saint living in Damascus is his real Sheikh , and that he is the only one who had the power to train him further. His name was Grandsheikh Abdullah Dagestani.

The time came to meet his real Sheikh..

Sheikh Nazim left immediately for Damascus to meet Grandsheikh Abdullah. This was during World War II, so the journey took 1 year..

**On the way to Damascus, Sheikh Nazim stopped and stayed at the Maqam of Khalid bin Walid (Ra) at Homs, where he learnt the 7 styles of Quran recitation. At that time Homs was a heavy War zone.**

**During prayer times, no one would go upto the Minaret to give Azan (call to prayer) because Bombs were dropped all over Homs. However, Sheikh Nazim went upto the Minaret and called the Azan.**

**From what he saw from the Minaret, Sheikh Nazim said: I witnessed an extraordinary sight, I saw the angels catching the bombs and throwing them back to where they came from.**
